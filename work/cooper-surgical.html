§ Project 01

A data spine leadership can use live.

Objective

Annual planning at a global medical-device business is a needle-threading exercise between top-line growth targets, sales-force capacity, and product-mix reality. The team required a data spine that could support all of these facets while enabling predictive and prescriptive analytics.

Approach

I own the analytics behind the Annual Operating Plan end-to-end: quota allocation across 75+ territory managers, ensemble forecasting across 25+ product lines, and the pipelines and executive surfaces that hold all of it together for the Medical Device business. No single model owns the truth — the forecast is a committee, and the committee shows its work.

  • Rebuilt the AOP process end-to-end, from transaction records to executive readouts
  • Created a PowerBI / SQL / Azure surface that replaced ad-hoc pulls with multiple live views
  • Standardized ensemble forecasting (ARIMA, SARIMAX, Prophet, boosting algorithms) across 25+ product lines
  • Ran scenario and sensitivity analysis to stress-test decisions before they hit a slide

Outcome

The AOP now runs on a data spine that leadership can use live. Product-launch decisions get vetted against the forecasting stack before they become commitments, and executive reporting time collapsed from days of ad-hoc pulls into real-time performance monitoring. The executive team trusts the numbers because they accurately reflect the work — every forecast and scenario model tags the assumptions it hangs on and states its confidence plainly.

What I'd do next

A closed loop between the forecast and the sales system so drift is caught within a cycle instead of at year-end reconciliation. And more automated attribution — separating pricing, mix, and volume effects at the product-line level, so when a number moves, we know why.
