§ Project 03

A globe you can sit with.

Personal build · Design, engineering, editorial · 2025 – 2026 · Essay

Next.js 14 · TypeScript · Three.js (WebGL) · Upstash Redis — sourced from UNHCR, OCHA, Reuters, HRW, GDELT 2.0, ACLED

The problem

The news doesn't invite you to sit. Caring at internet scale isn't really possible, and the fleeting helplessness of watching the world burn compresses into what psychologists call psychic numbing — the pattern where suffering, past a certain volume, stops registering as human at all. A statistic is what's left of a person after you've stopped paying attention.

Approach

Numit is a contemplative globe that maps two faces of the world. The shadow: places that are breaking right now. The light: some of the same places being put back together. You encounter the crisis first, and the response second — because hope handed to us before we've sat with the weight of something is decorative. Hope we arrive at afterwards is different. It lands as relief.

  • Hand-built WebGL globe in Three.js, not a wrapper library — every arc, every marker, every camera transition is deliberate
  • Two data planes: a curated set hand-checked against UNHCR, OCHA, Reuters, and Human Rights Watch; and a live stream from GDELT 2.0 and ACLED
  • NewsGuard-style credibility filter with a 75+ outlet threshold — an unverified story doesn't reach the surface
  • Representation-floor validation in CI so no crisis silently drops off the globe as news volume shifts
  • Transparent compositing: any person described is a disclosed composite from real reporting, never fictional
  • Engagement metrics aggregated through Upstash Redis — the site never watches individuals

Outcome

There isn't a conversion metric on Numit, and there was never going to be one. The success criterion is qualitative: if one person leaves having chosen a place, learned a name, and done one small real thing, the whole experience has paid for itself. The full essay lives on Substack.

What I'd do next

Regional editorial rotations so the shadow layer surfaces underreported crises, not just the ones with news volume. And an offline reading mode for places with unstable connectivity — the site should still work where the news does.
