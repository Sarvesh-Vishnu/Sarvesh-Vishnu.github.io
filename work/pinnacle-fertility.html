§ Project 04

Reading patient experience at scale.

Pinnacle Fertility · Data Scientist, Duke Fuqua capstone · Mar – May 2024

patient records
100K+
facilities covered
12+

Python · NLP · Classification · Tableau

Objective

Pinnacle operates twelve fertility facilities, and the patient-experience signal was hidden in unstructured feedback. Surveys, notes, and comments piled up faster than they could be processed — with no clear way to say, at scale, what was truly supporting or hurting patient satisfaction, and where.

Approach

I built the NLP layer end-to-end: cleaned and tokenized 100K+ patient records, trained sentiment models with facility-level and touchpoint-level cuts, and added a predictive component so satisfaction wasn't only measured after the fact. The goal wasn't a smarter dashboard — it was moving the team from lagging indicators to leading ones.

  • Ingested and cleaned patient records across 12 facilities into a consistent schema
  • Ran sentiment classification with facility-level and interaction-type breakdowns
  • Extracted drivers — the operational issues that materially move satisfaction, not the noise
  • Trained a predictive layer to flag interactions likely to go wrong before they did
  • Left the team with a framework for personalized care interventions, not just a report

Outcome

The team walked away with a facility-level ranking of what actually drives satisfaction, and a way to flag interactions at risk of going wrong before they did — moving from operational lagging indicators to operational leading ones.

What I'd do next

An A/B testing loop. Pair each sentiment prediction with a specific intervention and measure whether the intervention actually moves the outcome — not just whether it correlates with better sentiment. That's the only way to close the loop from insight to action.
