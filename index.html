Two threads, one practice.

  6. 2024 Domain breadth. NLP capstone with Pinnacle Fertility; marketing analytics at Duke's Nicholas School.
  7. 2025 ML in production. Asset-failure prediction for the Western Virginia Water Authority.
  8. 2025 → Commercial & executive. Cooper Surgical: Data Scientist in Commercial Excellence, then Senior Data Scientist in Strategy & Market Insights.

I work where data science meets strategy. The work I value most is where both meet people: a sales team’s targets, a city’s water mains, a patient’s experience of care.

The throughline: technical depth that survives a C-suite conversation, and a preference for pragmatic decisions over clever models. The best models I've built have always come out of taking a non-technical question seriously first.

Outside work I volunteer in animal welfare. In college I led a 150-person non-profit working towards the UN Sustainable Development Goals. I read social science alongside the statistics: causal inference, behavioural economics and strategy.
