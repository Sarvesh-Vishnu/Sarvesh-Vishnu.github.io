  8. 2025 → Commercial & executive. Cooper Surgical: Data Scientist in Commercial Excellence, then Senior Data Scientist in Strategy & Market Insights.

I work at the intersection of data, analytics, and strategy — and the more interesting bits are usually where that intersection runs into business, social sciences, and the messier parts of the world.

The throughline: technical depth that survives a C-suite conversation, and a preference for pragmatic decisions over clever models. The best models I've built have always come out of taking a non-technical question seriously first.

Outside the dashboards, I volunteer in animal welfare and led a 150-person socio-economic non-profit through college. I read more social science than is strictly necessary for the job — causal inference, behavioural econ, the quieter end of strategy.
